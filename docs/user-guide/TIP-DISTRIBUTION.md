# Tip distribution

Tip distribution calculates how pooled tips for a shift should be split across staff using configured weights, then saves an official distribution record. Managers often use the same screen from Tip distribution in the sidebar.

### Tip distribution screen

1. Tap Tip distribution in the sidebar.
2. Select a shift and business date at the top.
3. Load tips calculates totals from paid orders in that shift window.

![Tip distribution screen.](images/en/tips-overview.png)

*Tip distribution screen.*

### Shift and date

1. Choose the shift from the dropdown.
2. Pick the date — cannot be in the future.
3. Tap Load tips to fetch tip totals and build the distribution table.

![Shift, date, and load controls.](images/en/tips-filters.png)

*Shift, date, and load controls.*

### Distribution table

1. Total tips shows the pooled amount for the shift.
2. Each row lists user, role, weight, and calculated share.
3. Submit saves the distribution after you review the shares.

![Totals and per-user distribution table.](images/en/tips-table.png)
